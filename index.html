Behind the Creative Process

Creating something from scratch is one of my favorite things to do. I enjoy coming up with ideas, finding inspiration, choosing materials, and watching everything come together. I also want to share the steps I take throughout my projects so others can follow along and try making something themselves!

About Cam

Hi! I'm Cam, I'm 24 years old, and I was born and raised in Kansas City, Missouri. I currently work as a Shift Supervisor at Starbucks, but outside of work, I love finding ways to express myself creatively.

Some of my favorite things include creating art, traveling, spending time with friends, and thrifting. I love finding inspiration in everyday things and turning those ideas into something of my own.

I believe art is a window to the soul, and I hope this website gives you an inside look at my creativity, personality, and the projects I love making!
